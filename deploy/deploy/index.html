FAQs

Common Questions

What areas do you cover?+
East London to Essex — Stratford, Ilford, Romford, Barking, Leyton, Dagenham, Walthamstow, Forest Gate, Hackney, Bow and more. If you're not sure, just ask.
How quickly can you come out?+
For most jobs we can come out same day or next day. Emergency call-outs — burst pipes, leaks, blockages — are always prioritised.
Do you do full bathroom refurbishments?+
Yes — we handle complete bathroom refits including sanitaryware supply and fitting, plumbing, tiling, and all finishing work.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured?+
Yes — all our plumbers are licensed, insured, and highly trained professionals.
Do you charge a call-out fee?+
Only on emergencies, jobs outside East London and Essex, or out-of-hours work. Standard jobs are quoted upfront with no surprises.
Do you work for landlords and letting agents?+
Yes — we work with landlords and letting agents across East London and Essex, offering flexible scheduling, void property turnarounds, compliance checks and ongoing maintenance contracts with monthly invoicing and job reports.
Do you work with landlords and estate agents regularly?+
Yes — a good chunk of our work comes from landlords and letting agents who need a reliable team they can call whenever something comes up. No complicated contracts needed — just call us when you need us.
Can you do void property turnarounds quickly?+
Yes. We know empty properties cost money, so we prioritise void turnarounds and aim to get everything done in a single visit where possible. We'll send you photos and a report when the job's done.
Can you look after multiple properties for us?+
Absolutely — we work with landlords and agents managing anything from one property to a larger portfolio. Give us a call and we'll find an arrangement that works for you.
Are you insured and compliant?+
Yes — we hold public liability insurance and our operatives hold CSCS cards. We can provide documentation on request, which most letting agents and managing agents require before instructing a tradesperson.
